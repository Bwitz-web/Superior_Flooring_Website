Do you offer free flooring estimates?

Yes. Superior Flooring offers free quotes. Use the estimate form below or call 402-718-2093 to discuss your project.

What types of flooring do you install?

We work with hardwood, luxury vinyl plank, glue down planks, sheet vinyl and carpet, including commercial carpet. Tell us what you have in mind and we’ll discuss the options for your space.

Can you help with commercial spaces?

Yes. Our services include commercial carpet and durable plank flooring for business spaces. Share how the space is used so we can talk through the project.

What should I include in my estimate request?

Your location, the rooms you want to update, approximate size if known, and the flooring you’re considering are useful starting points. You can also let us know your preferred timing.

When can I get in touch?

Our regular hours are Monday through Friday, 9am–5pm. Saturday and Sunday appointments are available by arrangement.
